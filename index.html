RULES OF THE ROAD

SURVIVE

Health — If it reaches zero, the wasteland claims you. Some paths will hurt you without proper protection.

Supplies — Travel consumes what you carry. Find more to keep going.

Items — Collect gear to unlock safe paths. You'll need at least 3 useful items to gain entry at the Haven.

Choices — Hover over options to see what they cost. Grayed-out choices mean you cannot afford them right now.
